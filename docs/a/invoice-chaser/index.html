What it does

Tell it who owes you, what the invoice is and how late it is. It writes three reminders: one for today, one for a week later and a final one for two weeks later, each a little firmer, plus a 30-second phone script and three things not to say. The first reminder is saved as a draft in your Gmail. Nothing is sent.

You provide: the client, the invoice details, your name and business, and (optional) the relationship and a payment link.

You get: three emails, a phone script, and a Gmail draft of the first one.

Limits: it never threatens legal action, late fees or stopping work unless you wrote that yourself. It is not legal advice.

Sample output

Situation

Invoice 1042 for $2,400 was due Oct 30 and is now 16 days late.
Dana at Example Design Studio is a good client and this is the first time they are late, so start friendly.

Email 1, send today

Subject: Invoice 1042: a quick reminder

Hi Dana,

I hope all is well. A quick note that invoice 1042 for $2,400 was due Oct 30 and still shows as open on my side. It may simply have slipped through.

You can pay here: https://pay.example.com/1042

If it is already on its way, please ignore this note. Thank you!

Alex
Bright Studio

Email 2, send in 7 days if unpaid

Subject: Invoice 1042 for $2,400 is still open

Hi Dana,

I am following up on invoice 1042 for $2,400, which was due Oct 30 and is still unpaid.

Could you tell me the date you will send payment? You can pay here: https://pay.example.com/1042

If something is holding it up, tell me and we will sort it out together.

Alex
Bright Studio

Email 3, send in 14 days if unpaid

Subject: Final reminder: invoice 1042 for $2,400

Hi Dana,

This is my final reminder about invoice 1042 for $2,400, which was due Oct 30. I have written twice and have not had a payment or a date.

Please pay it this week, or reply today with the date you will pay. You can pay here: https://pay.example.com/1042

If there is a problem with the invoice or the work, tell me now so we can fix it.

Alex
Bright Studio

Phone script

"Hi Dana, it's Alex from Bright Studio. I'm calling about invoice 1042 for $2,400. It was due Oct 30 and I have not seen it come in. Is anything holding it up on your side?"
Listen first. Then ask: "What date can I note down for the payment?"

Do not say

  • "You always pay late." It is not true: this is their first time.
  • "I really need this money." It makes a business matter personal.
  • "This is your last chance." It sounds like a threat, and you have not decided on any next step.
