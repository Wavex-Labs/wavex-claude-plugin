What it does

Paste your numbers for this month and last month, your wins, your lows and what you need. It builds the metrics table with month-over-month change and runway, shows the math, flags numbers that look off, and writes a short update investors will actually read. The update is saved as a draft in your Gmail. Nothing is sent.

You provide: company name, metrics, wins, and (optional) lows, asks and who it goes to.

You get: a metrics table with the sums shown, and an update of 300 words at most as a Gmail draft.

Limits: it uses only the numbers you give and does not forecast. Check every figure before you send.
