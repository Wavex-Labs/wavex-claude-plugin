What it does

Paste what the client asked for and your services with prices. It restates the client's problem, sets what is in and out of scope, builds a pricing table using only your own prices, shows the sums, and writes a one to two page proposal plus a short cover email.

You provide: the client's brief, your services and prices, your business name, and (optional) payment terms and timeline.

You get: scope, assumptions, questions to ask the client, a pricing table with totals, the proposal text and a cover email to copy.

Limits: it never invents a price; a service the client needs that is not on your list is flagged for you to price. It is not a contract.

Proposal: workshop booking page and product photos

For Sam, from Alex at Bright Studio

What you asked for

You run a small online candle shop and you are adding candle-making workshops. You need a booking page so people can reserve a workshop seat, and 10 product photos for the shop, all before March 1. You also asked about a short video for Instagram.

What we will deliver

  • One booking page where people can reserve a workshop seat.
  • 10 product photos for your shop.

What is not included

  • Ongoing updates after launch. See the optional Monthly care plan below.
