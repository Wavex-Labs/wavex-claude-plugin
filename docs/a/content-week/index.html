What it does

Give your niche, your audience and one goal for the week. It first picks three themes that link what your audience cares about to your goal, then writes a plan for seven days. Each day has a format (Reel, carousel or story), a hook, what to show, a caption you can post as it is, and one call to action.

You provide: your niche and audience, this week's goal, and (optional) what you have on hand to film or show, and your voice.

You get: the week at a glance as a table, seven day cards, and a short list of what to do and what to confirm before day 1.

Limits: it plans and writes. It does not post, schedule or make images or video. It uses only what you give: no made-up prices, results, reviews, numbers or urgency. Where a fact is missing it writes [confirm: ...] for you to fill in.

Day 1

Format: Reel
Hook: Half a day on the water, start to finish.
Show: Phone clips from last weekend's trips, cut in the order of the day: leaving the dock, open water, the ride back.
Caption: Half a day is enough. You leave the dock, the town gets small, and the afternoon is yours. This is last weekend, with nothing added.
Call to action: Save this for your trip.

Day 2

Format: Story
Hook: Meet the captain who takes you out.
Show: A few story frames of the captain on the boat: a hello to camera, the checks before a trip, then a question box.
Caption: This is [confirm: the captain's name]. The person at the helm matters, so ask anything you would want to know before a day on the water.
Call to action: Put your question in the box.

Day 3

Format: Carousel
Hook: What a private half day looks like, part by part.
Show: Stills taken from last weekend's clips, in order: the hook over the boat, leaving the dock, open water, the stop at [confirm: where the trip stops], the ride back.
Caption: A private half-day trip, part by part. Your group, the boat, and an unhurried stretch of the day. What is included: [confirm: what the trip includes].
Call to action: Send this to the person you would bring.

Day 4

Format: Reel
Hook: Your group, your boat, nobody else on board.
Show: The guest who agreed to be filmed, on board with their group. This clip is not filmed yet: film it before Day 4, or use last weekend's clips of a group on deck.
Caption: Private means the boat is yours for the half day. Bring the people you came here with and keep the afternoon to yourselves.
Call to action: Message us to ask about a weekend date for your group.

Day 5

Format: Story
Hook: Tonight's sunset trip, as it happens.
Show: Story frames filmed during Friday's sunset trip: boarding, the light changing, the captain at the helm.
Caption: We are out for sunset this evening. Follow along as the light changes.
Call to action: Reply to this story with a question.

Day 6

Format: Carousel
Hook: Questions small groups ask before a boat day.
Show: A question on each slide with a short answer under it: is it private (yes), how many people ([confirm: the largest group you take]), what to bring ([confirm: what guests should bring]). Use stills from last weekend's clips behind the text.
Caption: The questions small groups ask before a boat day, answered plainly. If yours is not here, ask us and we will add it.
Call to action: Send us the question we missed.

Day 7

Format: Reel
Hook: Next weekend could look like this.
Show: The best few seconds from Friday's sunset trip, then the guest clip, ending on the boat at the dock.
Caption: Your group, the boat, half a day. If you are in town on a weekend, this is a calm way to spend part of it.
Call to action: Message us to ask about a weekend date for your group.

  • Agree a time to film the guest for Day 4.
  • Decide who films during Friday's sunset trip for Day 5.
