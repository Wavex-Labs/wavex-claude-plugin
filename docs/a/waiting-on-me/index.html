What it does

Your Claude scans your Gmail inbox for the last few days and finds threads where a real person wrote last and you have not answered. It ranks them: reply today, reply this week, no reply needed, with the reason and a one-line suggested answer. Then, only for the ones you approve, it saves reply drafts in Gmail. Nothing is sent.

You provide: how many days to look back, and (optional) who always matters and what to ignore.

You get: a ranked table and draft replies for the top few.

Needs: Gmail connected to your Claude. Run it in Claude chat or Claude Code. Your own Claude reads the emails; it never sends, deletes, archives or labels. The ranked summary (senders, subjects, one line each) is saved in your WaveX runs like any result.
