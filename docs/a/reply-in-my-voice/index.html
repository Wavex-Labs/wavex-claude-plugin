What it does

Paste the email you received and type what you want to say back, in a few words. It reads what the sender is really asking, checks that your answer covers each point, and writes the reply the way you write. The reply is saved as a draft in your Gmail. Nothing is sent: you read it and press send yourself.

You provide: the email, your answer in a few words, and (optional) one email you wrote before so it can match your voice.

You get: a short list of what they asked, anything you should confirm before sending, and the reply as a Gmail draft.

Limits: one reply per run. It only creates a draft; it does not read your inbox.

What they asked

  2. Whether the 10% discount is still on: answered

Check before you send

  • ☐ "Yes to 3 pm Thursday" is a time you are committing to. Check that 3 pm Thursday works on your side.
  • ☐ "delivery is free" is a money commitment. Check that you mean free delivery for this order.
  • ☐ The reply promises to come back on the invoice copy. Do that, or delete the line.
