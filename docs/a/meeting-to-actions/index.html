What it does

Paste your meeting notes or a transcript. It pulls out only what was really said: the decisions, each action item with its owner and due date, open questions and blockers. Every action item carries a short quote from your notes so you can check it. Then it writes the recap email and saves it as a draft in your Gmail. Nothing is sent.

You provide: the notes, and what the meeting was about.

You get: a clean record you can paste anywhere, and a recap email draft.

Limits: it invents no tasks, owners or dates; where the notes do not say, it writes 'unassigned' or 'no date'. Very long transcripts should be pasted in parts.
