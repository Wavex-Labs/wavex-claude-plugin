What it does

Paste the words of your landing page from top to bottom, say what you sell and who lands there. It scores the page on eight things a visitor decides on, quoting your own lines as evidence, then rewrites the top of the page and ranks the five fixes that would move the most.

You provide: the page text, your offer and price, who visits and the one action you want.

You get: a scorecard out of 40, a new headline, subhead and button text, three more headline options, five ranked fixes, and one rewritten section.

Limits: it reads words, not design, speed or images. It promises no conversion lift.
