What it does

Give a topic and your audience. It writes a carousel that people swipe: a hook slide, one idea per slide, and a closing slide with your call to action, plus the caption and hashtags. Then your Claude turns that copy into a design in your own Canva, ready to edit. With one of your brand kits, Canva makes options and only the one you pick is saved. Without a brand kit, Canva makes one design, and your Claude asks before it does.

You provide: topic, audience, and (optional) call to action, number of slides and style.

You get: the slide copy, a caption, and an editable Canva design.

Needs: Canva connected to your Claude. Run it in Claude chat or Claude Code. Nothing is posted or published anywhere: you export and post it yourself.

Your carousel copy

1. Slides

  1. Your photos are costing you sales
  1. Mistake 1: Dark, uneven light

Shoppers can't judge what they can't see. Shoot next to a window in daylight and turn the lamps off.

  1. Mistake 2: A busy background

Clutter pulls the eye away from the product. Use one plain surface and one plain wall.

  1. Mistake 3: Only one angle

Online, nobody can pick it up. Show the front, the back, the side and one close detail.

  1. Mistake 4: No sense of size

People guess the size and guess wrong. Add one photo with a hand or an everyday object.

  1. Mistake 5: Every photo looks different

Mixed crops and colors make a shop look careless. Pick one crop and one background, and keep them.

  1. Save this for later

Check your next five photos against this list.

2. Caption

Good products lose sales to bad photos. Here are five mistakes small shops make, and the simple fix for each: light, background, angles, size and consistency. None of them needs a new camera. Pick the one you see most in your own shop and fix it this week. Save this for later.

3. Hashtags

#productphotography #productphotos #onlineshopowner #smallshoptips #ecommercephotos

4. Alt text

A seven-slide carousel that lists five product photo mistakes small online shops make, with a short fix for each.
