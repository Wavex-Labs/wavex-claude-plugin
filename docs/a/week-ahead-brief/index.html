What it does

Your Claude reads the next 7 days of your Google Calendar and compares it with the three things you say must move this week. You get a one-screen brief: where the week is overloaded, meetings that clash or have no agenda, what to prepare, and free blocks worth protecting for focused work.

You provide: your top priorities, and (optional) your working hours and how much focus time you want.

You get: the week in five lines, a day-by-day view, up to five suggested fixes and a prep list.

Needs: Google Calendar connected to your Claude. Run it in Claude chat or Claude Code. It only reads your calendar; it creates, moves or declines nothing unless you say yes to each change afterwards. The brief is saved in your WaveX runs like any result.
