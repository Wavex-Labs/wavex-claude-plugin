What it does

Paste your latest reviews. It reads each one, notes how the person feels and the specific thing they mention, then writes one public reply per review in your voice. It ends with the short list of reviews that need a personal follow-up, and what to do first. Nothing is posted for you: you copy each reply where you want it.

You provide: your business name and what you offer, the reviews (one per line), and (optional) your voice and how an unhappy guest can reach you.

You get: a numbered reply for every review, in the order you pasted them, then the follow-up list.

Limits: up to 20 reviews a run. It never offers a refund, a discount or anything free, never promises a fix, never argues, and does not confirm a complaint you have not checked yet. It uses only what the reviews and you say. It does not read or post to any review site: you paste and you copy.
