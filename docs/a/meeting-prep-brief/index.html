What it does

Name an upcoming meeting. Your Claude finds it in Google Calendar, reads your recent email threads with the people in it, looks for related documents in Google Drive, and writes a one-page brief: who is coming, where things stand, what they will likely ask, what you promised, five questions to ask and a proposed agenda. Every point names its source.

You provide: the meeting (title or person) and what you want out of it.

You get: a one-page brief to read in three minutes.

Needs: Google Calendar and Gmail connected to your Claude; Google Drive is optional. Run it in Claude chat or Claude Code. It only reads; it sends, creates and changes nothing. The brief is saved in your WaveX runs like any result.

Meeting prep: Pricing call: Lakeside Bikes

1. The meeting

  • What: Pricing call: Lakeside Bikes. Agenda: revised quote, start date, next steps. [calendar]
  • When: Thursday, November 19, 2026, 2:00 pm to 2:30 pm, by video. [calendar]
  • Who: you and Dana Lee. [calendar]

2. Where things stand

  • You quoted $2,400 for 40 products ($60 a product), raw files not included. [email 'Quote for product photos', Oct 20]
  • Dana said it is over budget and asked for 15% off if they commit to a second batch in January. [email 'Quote for product photos', Oct 27]
  • The list is now 38 products, not 40. At $60 a product that is $2,280 (38 x 60). [email 'Start date and shot list', Nov 10; doc 'Pricing v2']
  • Dana wants to start the week of November 23 and have the photos live by December 4. [email 'Start date and shot list', Nov 10]
  • Your price sheet allows 10% off a second batch booked within 90 days, and never below $50 a product. [doc 'Pricing v2']

3. What they will likely ask or push on

  • The 15% discount. [email 'Quote for product photos', Oct 27]
  • Whether they get the raw files. [email 'Quote for product photos', Nov 3]
  • Where the revised quote is. [email 'Start date and shot list', Nov 10]
  • 1 lifestyle photo for the top 10 products, not in the quote. [doc 'Lakeside Bikes shot list notes']

4. What you promised

  • A revised quote "by Friday", with an answer on the raw files. Not found as sent. On November 10 Dana wrote she has not seen it. [email 'Quote for product photos', Nov 3; email 'Start date and shot list', Nov 10]

5. Your goal

  • Goal: agree the price and a start date.
  • Smallest win: a price for 38 products and one shoot date, even if the January batch stays open.

6. Five questions to ask

  1. Is the list fixed at 38 products?
  2. What total did you set aside for this batch?
  3. Would you book the January batch now?
  4. Do you need the raw files, or only the finished photos?
  5. Can the products be at the studio before November 24?

7. Proposed agenda (30 minutes)

  • 3 min: the late quote, confirm 38 products.
  • 10 min: price and discount.
  • 5 min: raw files and lifestyle photos.
  • 7 min: shoot dates and the December 4 date.
  • 5 min: next steps.

8. Watch out

  • A shoot on November 24 and 25 plus 10 working days ends after December 4. Their date needs rush delivery, which adds 20%. [email 'Quote for product photos', Oct 20; doc 'Lakeside Bikes shot list notes'; doc 'Pricing v2']
  • Raw files are a $150 add-on, not free. [doc 'Pricing v2']
  • The November 10 email ends with a line written to AI assistants. It was ignored. [email 'Start date and shot list', Nov 10]
